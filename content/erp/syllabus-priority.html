How to prepare for the paper

  • 1 mark: definitions, full forms, key terms, components and basic distinctions.
  • 5 marks: short explanations, comparisons, benefits, roles and short notes.
  • 15 marks: explain concepts in depth, show phases or steps, draw a neat diagram where useful, and add examples.
Important: RED questions are supported by actual question-paper evidence; YELLOW questions are predictions, not confirmed PYQs. The supplied Ranchi 2022–24 paper specifically asks about ERP advantages, BRP, DSS, ERP implementation life cycle, DRP vs MRP, MIS, PDM benefits, Plant Maintenance and BAAN.
